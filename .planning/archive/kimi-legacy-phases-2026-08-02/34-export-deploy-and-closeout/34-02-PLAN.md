# Phase 34-02 Plan: Deployment Baseline

## Steps

1. Audit TLS, secret management, backup, monitoring, and cost-alert configuration.
2. Separate local configuration evidence from production readiness claims.
3. Record missing approvals and remediation owners.

## Must-Haves

- No secrets are emitted.
- No deployment, remote write, or production configuration mutation occurs without authorization.
- Cost alerts use a real pricing authority or remain explicitly unverified.

## Verification

- Read-only audit of Compose, tunnel configuration, environment-file handling, backups,
  health endpoints, monitoring files, and pricing/cost evidence.
- Findings are redacted and distinguish local evidence from production readiness.

## Test, Fix, and Confirm

The read-only audit is complete. Production TLS verification, default credential removal,
monitoring/alerting, backup ownership/restore evidence, pricing authority, and any deployment
change remain blocked or partial pending explicit authorization and external evidence.
