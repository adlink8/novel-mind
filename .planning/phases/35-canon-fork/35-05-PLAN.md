## Must-Haves

- Branch/fork scope and SkillRun/ToolRun/Artifact lineage are mandatory.
- Agent output is candidate-only; server-side Validator and ApprovalRequest fail closed.
- `backend/app/services/canon_fork/materializer.py` is the sole owner of approved fork materialization; `backend/app/api/canon_fork.py` is the sole HTTP delegation boundary for this plan.
- Materialization requires the exact approved `create_canon_fork` action and matching proposal/delta payload hash; it cannot modify Original Canon or active pointer.
