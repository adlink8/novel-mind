## Must-Haves

- Branch/fork scope and SkillRun/ToolRun/Artifact lineage are mandatory.
- Agent output is candidate-only; server-side Validator and ApprovalRequest fail closed.
- A divergence path must complete `allow_divergence` approval, full revalidation, and a separate `publish_derivative_revision` approval in that order.
- Both ApprovalRequests must bind the same exact `draft_hash` and `canon_delta_hash`; approval reuse, hash drift or skipped revalidation fails closed.
- Deterministic services alone publish the approved derivative revision and can never write Original Canon.
