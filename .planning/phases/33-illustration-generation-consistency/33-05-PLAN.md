## Must-Haves

- The only Phase 33 IllustrationRevision states are `candidate`, `validated`, and `proposal_ready`, in that order.
- Phase 33 creates no ApprovalRequest, calls no publisher, and never writes or exposes `published`; Phase 34 owns approval and deterministic publication.
- Branch/fork scope and SkillRun/ToolRun/Artifact lineage are mandatory, and the deterministic validator is the only Phase 33 authority that may advance status.
- The proposal-ready handoff is read-only and rejects stale, non-proposal-ready or wrong-scope revisions.
