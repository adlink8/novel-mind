## Must-Haves

- Descent follows only legal visible edges and direct claim source links under one immutable RetrievalScope.
- Mixed mode is a bounded union of genuinely different visible levels with deterministic leaf dedupe.
- Missing/partial/invalid upper memory collapses downward and then raw-falls back without widening any scope or cutoff.
- Final citations are created exclusively by fresh `Chapter.content[start:end]` re-slice and exact hash/lineage validation.
- Upper claims, summaries, labels, scores, embeddings, snippets and chat text are never citations.
- Retrieval manifests contain only visible metadata and distinguish post-budget omission from hidden future data by never observing the latter.
